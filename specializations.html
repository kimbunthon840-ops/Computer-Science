Machine Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence & Deep Learning

Build neural systems that generalize from vast datasets. Learn mathematical optimization, backpropagation, and transformer attention.

Key Core Concepts

  • Matrix calculus, gradient descent, Adam optimizer
  • Convolutional Networks & Computer Vision
  • Multi-head self-attention & Transformer architecture
  • RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback)

Defensive & Offensive

Cybersecurity & Cryptography

Protect digital infrastructure and analyze threat models. Explore mathematical cryptosystems, reverse engineering, and low-level exploit analysis.

Key Core Concepts

  • Symmetric (AES-256-GCM) & Asymmetric (RSA, Ed25519)
  • Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZK-SNARKs) & Hash functions
  • Memory corruption: Stack buffer overflows & ROP chains
  • Zero-Trust network architecture & identity federation

Ghidra Wireshark Rust Burp Suite

Planet-Scale Systems

Cloud & Distributed Systems

Orchestrate fault-tolerant clusters serving hundreds of millions of concurrent queries. Master consensus, replication, and high-availability patterns.

Key Core Concepts

  • CAP Theorem & PACELC trade-offs
  • Consensus protocols: Raft & Paxos leader election
  • Eventual consistency & Vector Clocks
  • Microservices, gRPC protobufs, and distributed tracing
Industry Stack:
Kubernetes Docker Terraform Kafka

Physics & Logic

Quantum Computing

Harness quantum mechanical phenomena—superposition and entanglement—to achieve exponential speedups over classical Turing machines.

Key Core Concepts

  • Bloch sphere representation of Qubits
  • Quantum logic gates: Hadamard (H), Pauli-X, CNOT
  • Shor's polynomial-time integer factorization algorithm
  • Grover's quadratic unstructured database search
